About Sunrise Model School & College

Shaping young minds through knowledge, values and opportunities. Discover our journey, the people behind our institution and the vision that inspires everything we do.

OUR STORY

The History of Our School

Sunrise Model School & College is committed to creating an inspiring learning environment where students can discover their abilities, develop confidence and prepare for the future.

Our journey reflects a dedication to quality education, discipline, creativity and character development. We believe every student deserves the opportunity to learn, grow and achieve their full potential.

Through the dedication of our teachers, leadership and school community, we continue working towards a brighter future for every learner.
